We started BrewHaven with one simple belief — great coffee isn’t just a habit, it’s a feeling. Located in the heart of Kolkata at Park Street, our café is waiting for you.

Our coffee beans are sourced directly from plantations in Kerala, Coorg, and Indonesia, and are roasted in small batches by our master roasters — each cup is a blend of science and emotion.
